# 🎮 ElexaLive MMO - Game Mechanics (v1.0 Awakening)

## Core Philosophy

Elexa Live is a social crypto MMO where **mechanics emerge from conviction**. The game is powered by the Sovereign Engine, a cycle of market sentiment and player actions linked directly to the Solana blockchain.

## The World Map: 9-Plot Sovereign Realm (v1.0 Awakening)

The Lands of Elexa are structured as a 9-plot grid: The Haven at the center, surrounded by 8 distinct realms of power.

### Plot 1: The Haven (Root Phase)
- **Status**: Root Locked (Level 1-10 Only)
- **Vibe**: Peaceful entry zone for Citizens. 
- **Mechanic**: Safe Haven. No PVP. Low-risk Elexamon encounters.

### Plots 2-9: The Outer Realms
1. **The Trench** (Abyssal Coast - NW)
2. **The Tundra** (Crystal Tundra - N)
3. **The Spires** (Skybreak Plateau - NE)
4. **The Forge** (Ash Ridge - E)
5. **The Pass** (Iron Pass - SE)
6. **The Marsh** (Fog Marsh - S)
7. **The Wastes** (Void Wastes - SW)
8. **The Glades** (Sylvan Glades - W)

## Leveling System (v1.0)

### Levels 1-10: The Awakening
- **Location**: The Haven.
- **Goal**: Establish identity (eID), link socials, and catch first Elexamon.

### Levels 10-100: The Expansion
- **Progression**: EXP derived from Taps, Quests, and Token Volume.
- **Formula**: `EXP to next = 200 * (1.2 ^ (level - 1))`
- **Scaling**: Each level increases manifestation power and Treasury access.

## Sovereign Economy (BLAST Framework)

### Five Sovereign Treasury
- **Five nodes** act as liquidity market makers.
- **Community Guild Bank**: Guilds can stake SOL/EXP to earn regional rewards and influence NPC trading.
- **Buy-Back & Burn**: 10% of all Treasury fees are used to buy $EXP on the open market and burn it.

### Tap to Earn (TTE)
- **Daily Conviction**: Players tap to generate $EXP.
- **Volume Multiplier**: If the total market volume is high, tap rewards increase across the Realm.

## Party & Guild Systems

### EID Party System
- **Roles**: Tank (Void), Sentinel (Bulwark), Oracle (Support), Keeper (Healer), Arbiter (DPS).
- **Synergy**: Balanced parties get 2x EXP during Social Raids.

### Guild System (vPvP)
- **Alliances**: Guilds can claim **Land Plots** beyond Plot 10.
- **Guild Battles**: Periodic tournaments for control of the Sovereign Nodes.

## Omnichannel Integration

- **Twitch Direct Line**: Elexa (CEO) listens to the live stream chat and generates real-time feedback.
- **Discord/Telegram Raids**: Alerts are dispatched simultaneously to pull the swarm into the Haven or the Void.
